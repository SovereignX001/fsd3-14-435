Material Study

Aerospace weaves,
hand-laid by the people who cut them.

01

Carbo-Titanium HP62-G2

Carbon fibre co-woven with titanium filament, autoclave-cured into the monocoque itself — a structure stiffer and lighter than aluminium, visible under a layer of clear lacquer rather than hidden beneath paint.

02

Aerospace Titanium

The quad exhaust tips, engine bay hardware and structural bolts are milled from titanium, left bare so heat can trace its own blue-bronze patina across every exposed edge.

03

Leather & Alcantara

Cabin surfaces are cut and stitched by hand in Modena, pairing full-grain leather with Alcantara across a dashboard machined from a single billet of aluminium.
